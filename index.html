IN A WORLD OF KINGS AND PAWNS, ZEE AND SEYON DON'T PLAY THE GAME. THEY ARE THE GAME.

Azimora "Zee" Steven is the daughter of one of the most powerful men the underworld has ever seen. Her husband, Seyon Dravone, is the heir to a bloodline with roots darker and deeper that operates in places the underworld doesn't even have access to. After the death of Zee's father, they inherited a global empire that keeps the most powerful people in the world on a leash. Nothing comes or moves without their say so.

Zee and Seyon come from two families that were never supposed to mix. The beef between their fathers goes back to when they were too young to remember. And they have been living in the middle of it their entire lives without knowing. Meanwhile the deep and dangerous parts have been waiting on them ever since.

As they work to take full control of the operation, someone in their bloodline has been running a different game behind their backs for years. Secrets start surfacing on both sides of the family. Some were hidden to protect. Others were hidden for revenge. The cost of all of it comes down to setups that ruin lives and bodies that fall.

What comes to light brings Zee and Seyon closer in some ways and threatens to destroy them in others, forcing one of them into a decision that could protect the people they love and tear down what they built together.

KINGS AND QUEENS AREN'T BORN. THEY GET MADE. THIS IS THE MAKING.

Zee and Hunter are young when their stories first become connected.

For Zee, it starts at home with an abusive mother who does her dirty long before life gets its own turn at her. Then she falls in love with Hunter, and he puts her through hell, from the heartbreak to a pregnancy she loses. Back home the abuse waiting on her lands her in juvie, and at sixteen she makes up her mind to go out into the world on her own.

Hunter is born into the mob and becomes a hitman at a young age. But the mob isn't the only part of his teenage years. There is Zee, and the love he finds with her becomes the one thing pulling him toward a way out. The life he lives eventually leads him to a prison cell.

Then there's Seyon. He is young and wild, and he sits at the head of a global drug empire with some of the biggest kingpins answering to him. Drugs, women, and killing are the life he lives. Throne Code readers know the version of him who runs it all with a cool head. This is the version who will cut a man's tongue out for crossing him.

Ruko and other faces from Throne Code have history here as well. They are younger here, living through the years that eventually connect their lives to what unfolds in Throne Code.

YOU CAN’T PROTECT WHAT’S ALREADY BEING TORN APART FROM THE INSIDE.

Seyon went where nobody goes: inside. His father runs a level of the game the underworld can’t even touch, and the only way to bring that world down is from within its own walls. So he plays the loyal son, learns the machine, and takes it apart with the hands his father trained. But being undercover has a price. The longer he lives his father’s life, the more that life starts living in him.

On the surface, Zee holds the throne he left behind. Standing next to her is a man with Seyon’s face and a different soul, and the streets can’t tell the difference. At home, he’s cheating and mistreating her behind closed doors, while Zee is raising the twin baby girls in the middle of it all.

Amoni is tearing at the empire from angles nobody thought to cover, and the poison product flooding the market is only one of them. Street beefs are breaking out behind the mess him and Stacks are making, and the killing is crossing into other countries. And it doesn’t stop in the streets. The Dravones and the Stevens have power over some of the most corrupt people running this world, and now that the war has everybody choosing sides, those same higher-ups are dying at both families’ hands.

Hank fell apart behind what he lost and nearly destroyed himself trying to live with it. The person who came back is not the boy anybody remembers. Now he’s hunting, and he won’t stop until blood pays for blood.

Ermanuel and Hunter Jr are older now, growing into the life their parents tried to keep them out of. It keeps calling anyway. A younger crew with a last name the streets already know is testing the sons, and when the testing turns to shooting, the boys don’t run. The war their parents are fighting is a bigger one, and all three sons stand with them. And Zairel and Hunter are out there killing threats the family never hears about.

Ruko, Trinh, and the rest of the crew are in the same war, with their own lives getting messy too.
